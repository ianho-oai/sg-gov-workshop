GETTING STARTED

One track. One conversation.

  1. Open Choose your adventure to select one of the four government workflows, or choose Advanced API or 3D Singapore landmark.
  2. Complete step 0. The first four tracks use Gmail or Outlook; Advanced API uses a Codex workspace and secure API key setup.
  3. Read Your assignment in the separate tab after Setup to understand your role, the overall goal and expected outputs.
  4. Click the resources above each task to download them, then upload them to your conversation. Use one ChatGPT conversation for a standard track, or one Codex workspace for Advanced API.
  5. Read Your Task first. Choose Expand model answer when you want to see and copy the proposed prompt.
  6. Use the numbered tabs or left/right arrows to move through the steps.
  7. For the first four tracks: at step 2, upload the image, read the visual evidence and generate a brainstorm mind map. At step 4, upload the Excel workbook. At step 7, upload the new-data workbook to process a fresh batch and check that repeating it creates no duplicate drafts.
  8. At step 9, create a recurring source watch and verify its saved schedule and first check. This guide does not activate tasks itself.
